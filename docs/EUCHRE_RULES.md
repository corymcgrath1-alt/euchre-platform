# Euchre Rules

## Deck and seats

The engine uses a 24-card Euchre deck: 9, 10, J, Q, K, A in clubs, diamonds, hearts, and spades. Seats are `0` South, `1` West, `2` North, and `3` East. Teams are `0` for South/North and `1` for West/East.

## Bidding

Round one begins left of the dealer. A player may pass or order the dealer to pick up the upcard suit. If ordered up, the dealer takes the upcard, discards back to five, and the upcard suit becomes trump.

If all four pass, round two begins left of the dealer. Players may pass or call any suit except the turned-down upcard suit. Optional stick-the-dealer prevents the dealer from passing after the first three round-two passes.

## Bowers and effective suit

The jack of trump is the right bower and highest trump. The jack of the same-color suit is the left bower and its effective suit is trump. Follow-suit and trick ranking always use effective suit, not printed suit.

## Lone hands

When a caller goes alone, the caller's partner sits out. The leader skips the sitting partner, each trick receives one play from the three active seats, and the sitting partner retains their five cards. A lone march earns four points; a lone hand that wins three or four tricks earns one point.

## Scoring

- Makers win three or four tricks: makers score 1.
- Makers win all five tricks: makers score 2.
- Lone maker wins all five tricks: makers score 4.
- Makers win fewer than three tricks: defenders score 2 for a euchre.
- Match ends when a team reaches the configured target score, normally 10.
