## Description

Play a complete game of Euchre wherever you are.

Euchre Club is a focused solo card game for iPhone. Sit South against three deterministic
computer opponents and play every phase from the opening deal through bidding, trump,
dealer discard, trick play, hand scoring, and the winning point.

Choose Easy, Standard, or Strong opponents. Set the target score, turn stick-the-dealer
on or off, select supported house rules, or enter a practice seed to repeat a deal.

Your active game is saved as you play. Leave the app, come back later, and resume from
the same ordered move history. Completed games remain in local history with mobile game
reviews.

Highlights:

- Complete rules-driven solo Euchre
- Three deterministic computer opponents
- Easy, Standard, and Strong difficulty
- Configurable target score and supported house rules
- Seeded practice games
- Offline play with local save and resume
- Completed-game history and review
- Optional haptics, reduced animation, and play confirmation
- Native sharing for final scores and practice seeds

Euchre Club version 1.0 has no account, ads, tracking, purchases, subscriptions, chat,
Ranked mode, or human multiplayer.

## App Review notes

This is an offline solo game. No account or network connection is required.

Suggested review flow:

1. Tap Play Solo.
2. Start a game with the default settings or set target score to 5 for a shorter game.
3. Use the highlighted engine-authorized bidding and card controls.
4. Background and reopen the app to verify local resume.
5. Open Game History after completing a game and choose Review Game.
6. Use Share Result to open the native iOS share sheet.

There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, external purchase links, accounts,
advertisements, analytics, user-generated content, chat, location services, or human
multiplayer.
