Privacy Policy

Euchre Club version 1.0 is designed as an offline solo card game. Game logic, saved games, settings, history, and reviews run on and remain on your device during normal play.

Data stored on your device

The app stores game records, ordered move events, active-game recovery data, completed-game history, practice seeds, reviews, and settings in local application storage. This data is used to play, resume, and review games.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app does not provide accounts or sign-in and does not include analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or advertising identifiers.

When information can leave the device

Core gameplay does not require a network connection. If you choose Share Result, iOS opens its native share sheet and you choose the destination. Opening a privacy or support link sends a normal web request to the selected site. GitHub Pages may process standard request metadata according to GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting local data

Reset Local Data removes saved games and settings after confirmation. Removing the app also removes its application-container data according to iOS behavior.

Permissions and device access

Version 1.0 does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, Bluetooth, notifications, or advertising identifiers. It uses lifecycle events, optional haptics, status-bar and launch-screen integration, and the native share sheet.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the public support page. Do not include private device information in a support request.
